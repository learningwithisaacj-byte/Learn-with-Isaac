Course Curriculum

4 modules, broken into short, practical lessons you can follow at your own pace.

Module 1: Channel Setup & Planning

5 lessons
  • Channel branding: setting up your channel, banner art, and channel trailer
  • Choosing a niche: picking a content direction you can sustain long-term
  • Scripting & storyboarding: planning a video before you hit record
  • Project: a complete channel setup and your first video script

Module 2: Filming on a Budget

5 lessons
  • Phone filming basics: lighting, framing, and stability without expensive gear
  • Audio basics: recording clear sound and avoiding common mistakes
  • On-camera confidence: tips for presenting naturally, even if you're camera-shy
  • Project: film your first raw video from script to footage

Module 3: Editing & Thumbnails

5 lessons
  • Basic editing: cuts, transitions, text overlays, and background music
  • Thumbnails & titles: designing click-worthy thumbnails in Canva
  • Pacing & structure: editing so viewers watch until the end
  • Project: edit your first video with a matching thumbnail

Module 4: Growth & Monetization

5 lessons
  • Discoverability: titles, tags, and descriptions that help videos get found
  • Reading Analytics: using YouTube Studio to see what's working
  • Monetization paths: AdSense, sponsorships, and memberships explained
  • Final project: a publish-ready video with a growth and posting plan
