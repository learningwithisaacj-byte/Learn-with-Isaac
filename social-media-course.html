Course Curriculum

4 modules, broken into short, practical lessons you can follow at your own pace.

Module 1: Planning & Strategy

5 lessons
  • Setting goals: defining what success looks like for an account
  • Platform best practices: what works differently on each platform
  • Content pillars: building a repeatable content strategy
  • Research: studying competitors and inspiration accounts

Module 2: Content Calendars & Scheduling

5 lessons
  • Monthly calendars: planning content themes and post types in advance
  • Batch creation: creating a week or month of content in one sitting
  • Scheduling tools: automating when and where posts go live
  • Cross-platform adapting: tailoring one piece of content for multiple platforms

Module 3: Captions, Hashtags & Engagement

5 lessons
  • Caption writing: hooks and calls-to-action that drive engagement
  • Hashtag research: finding and using effective hashtags
  • Community management: responding to comments and DMs professionally
  • Project: a week of captions and hashtag sets ready to post

Module 4: Analytics & Growth

5 lessons
  • Reading insights: understanding key metrics on each platform
  • Identifying top content: spotting what resonates with an audience
  • Adjusting strategy: using data to refine future content
  • Reporting: presenting results clearly, useful for client work
