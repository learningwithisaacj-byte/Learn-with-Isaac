Course Curriculum

4 modules, broken into short, practical lessons you can follow at your own pace.

Module 1: Getting Started as a Freelancer

5 lessons
  • Choosing your service: deciding what you'll offer as a freelancer
  • Platform profiles: setting up profiles on freelance platforms
  • Building a portfolio: presenting your work, even with limited experience
  • Setting first rates: pricing your services when starting out

Module 2: Finding & Pitching Clients

5 lessons
  • Where to find work: platforms, job boards, and warm outreach
  • Writing proposals: pitches that get replies instead of ignored
  • Following up: staying visible without being pushy
  • Project: write and send your first three pitches

Module 3: Contracts, Pricing & Getting Paid

5 lessons
  • Pricing models: hourly vs. project-based vs. value-based pricing
  • Simple contracts: writing a clear scope of work to protect both sides
  • Invoicing & payments: getting paid safely and on time
  • Handling scope creep: managing extra requests professionally

Module 4: Managing Clients & Growing

5 lessons
  • Onboarding clients: starting projects smoothly and setting expectations
  • Communication & deadlines: keeping projects on track without burning out
  • Referrals & reviews: turning happy clients into repeat business
  • Raising your rates: knowing when and how to charge more
