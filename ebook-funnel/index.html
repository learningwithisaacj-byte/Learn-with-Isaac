AI + Writing & Content

#1 — LinkedIn Ghostwriting for Thought Leaders

What You Sell
A weekly batch of LinkedIn posts, written in the client's own voice
Customer
Consultants, coaches, founders, B2B sales leaders
Startup Cost
Free to start; ~$20/month once you have a client
Pricing
$400–$1,500/month for 8 posts
Difficulty
★★☆☆☆
Weekend Feasibility
🟢 Can realistically be started this weekend
FridayPick your niche and identify 10 potential clients. Set up your AI tool and voice-to-text workflow.
SaturdayConduct 2–3 practice interviews and produce sample posts using your prompt.
SundaySend 5 personalized outreach messages with a sample post attached.
Role: You are an experienced LinkedIn ghostwriter who specializes in [INDUSTRY]. Context: Below is a transcript of an interview with my client, who works as a [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY TYPE]... Objective: Turn this transcript into one LinkedIn post of 150-200 words that sounds like a real person... Constraints: No emojis unless the transcript shows the client uses them. No hashtags... Output format: The post text only, followed by three alternative opening lines... Quality bar: This should read like something the client wrote themselves...

The Method

The Weekend Framework

The goal isn't to build a perfect company in 48 hours. It's to create something real enough to test with one real customer.

Friday

Choose & Prepare

Pick your idea, set up free AI tools, and identify a first list of real prospects.

Saturday

Build & Test

Produce one real, complete sample of the work — then review and edit it yourself before it goes anywhere.

Sunday

Launch & Reach Out

Send personalized outreach to real people, with your sample attached. Follow up with anyone who responds.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The book is written for non-technical readers — several ideas are explicitly beginner-friendly, and Chapter 2 helps you filter to the ones that match your actual skills and experience level.

Do I need ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro?

No. Free tiers are genuinely usable for testing an idea before you spend anything. If you later want to upgrade, a single $20/month subscription to one general-purpose AI tool covers the majority of ideas in the book.

Do I need any coding skills?

No. None of the 100 ideas require programming or technical development skills.

Are these passive-income ideas?

No. These are real businesses that need real customers. AI removes friction from the work — it doesn't remove the work itself.

Can I start these with little money?

Yes. Most ideas in the book can start for free or under $30, using free-tier AI tools before you spend anything.

Does the book guarantee I'll make money?

No, and it says so directly. You'll find realistic price ranges throughout, but no promised income figures — results depend on your market, effort, skill, and the usual factors no book can control.

How will I receive the eBook?

Instant digital download link after successful payment, on the confirmation page and by email.

Can I read it on my phone or tablet?

Yes. It's a standard digital PDF, readable on any phone, tablet, computer, or e-reader.

Will the AI tools mentioned in the book change over time?

Likely, yes. The book is upfront about this — tool names, prices, and platform policies reflect 2026 research and will shift. Always verify current pricing and features directly with the provider.
