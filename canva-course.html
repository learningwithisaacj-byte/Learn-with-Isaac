Course Curriculum

4 modules, broken into short, practical lessons you can follow at your own pace.

Module 1: Canva Basics & Workspace

5 lessons
  • Getting started: creating your Canva account and touring the dashboard
  • Templates & design types: finding and customizing the right starting point
  • Brand Kit setup: saving your colors, fonts, and logo for reuse
  • Resizing & repurposing: turning one design into versions for multiple platforms

Module 2: Designing Social Media Graphics

5 lessons
  • Instagram & Reels: designing posts, Stories, and Reels covers that stand out
  • Facebook & YouTube assets: cover photos, thumbnails, and banner ads
  • Content planning: using Canva's calendar and templates to plan a week of posts
  • Project: design a full week of on-brand social posts

Module 3: Elements, Text & Photo Editing

5 lessons
  • Typography: pairing fonts and building clean, readable layouts
  • Icons & illustrations: using Canva's element library effectively
  • Photo editing: background remover, filters, and image adjustments
  • Color theory basics: choosing palettes that match a brand's mood

Module 4: Branding & Client-Ready Design

5 lessons
  • Logo & brand kit: building a simple, complete brand identity in Canva
  • Exporting correctly: file types and settings for web, print, and social
  • Presenting your work: sharing designs and mockups with a client
  • Final project: a complete brand kit + social pack you can add to your portfolio
